Latinformatics Remote Support (LRS) Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

This policy describes how Latinformatics handles information in connection with Latinformatics Remote Support (LRS), including the Agent and Viewer applications.

Information We Process

LRS processes information necessary to authenticate users, authorize remote connections, manage subscriptions, and establish remote support sessions. This may include account information, device identifiers, connection information, and technical diagnostic records.

Remote Support Sessions

During an authorized remote support session, LRS transmits screen information and remote input commands in real time between the Agent and Viewer, using its supporting relay infrastructure as necessary. Latinformatics does not record or store session screen content.

How Information Is Used

Information is used to operate LRS, authenticate accounts, enforce access permissions, maintain security, troubleshoot technical problems, and provide customer support.

Information Sharing

Latinformatics does not sell personal information. Limited information may be processed by infrastructure and payment service providers as necessary to operate LRS, or disclosed when required by law.

Security

Latinformatics uses security measures designed to protect account information and remote connections. No system can guarantee absolute security.

Data Retention

Session screen content is not retained by Latinformatics. Account, authorization, subscription, and technical records may be retained as needed for service operation, security, and legal obligations.

Your Choices

You may request information about your personal data or ask for correction or deletion, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements.
